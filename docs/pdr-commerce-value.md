# PDR Commerce — the value proposition

> Settled 2026-07-26. Answers the hardest question: *"they link a shop, we say we'll run it — and
> what? what do we actually give them?"* Read with `pdr-commerce-outline.md` (what it does) and
> `pdr-competition.md` (who else).

## The rule

**Nobody pays for autonomy.** "It runs itself" is a mechanism, not a benefit — Icon.com proved the
market punishes that pitch. Value must be nameable in **money, risk, or hours**, and verifiable.

## What the customer buys (three things)

| | The comparison they make | Why only we can |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Agent distribution** — findable AND buyable by AI across every surface and standard | vs. invisible: losing orders they never see | Shopify syndicates only its own merchants, with no measurement or optimization loop. Nobody closes crawl → retrieval → order |
| **2 · Executed work** — this week's ads, posts, price fixes, restocks, margin report | vs. a €2–5k/mo agency retainer or a hire | The brain keeps output on-brand and product-accurate; the store is the same record, so there is no integration seam |
| **3 · A brain that compounds** — rules taught, outcomes learned, visual world, product specs | vs. starting over elsewhere | It is *their* company's operating knowledge — and the switching cost |

## The Shopify question: don't out-plugin them, plug into them

Shopify's moat is rails + 8,000 apps + trust. Unwinnable, and unnecessary. **"Link your shop" is
the distribution strategy, not a weakness** — Commerce is the operating layer *above* any
storefront (Shopify, Woo, or one Studio fabricated). Our ecosystem equivalent:

- **Connectors** — operate the tools they already pay for rather than replace them (breadth
  without 8,000 apps).
- **Workers as the extension point** — Sales · Support · Finance · Logistics · Analytics: our app
  store, first-party, one shared brain. Third-party workers later.
- **The brain as an API** — knowledge packs are the plugin analog (EU VAT pack, vertical packs, an
  agency's playbook sold as a pack).
- **Protocols over integrations** — UCP/ACP support and every store exposed as an **MCP server**,
  so any agent can shop it without a business-development deal. Standards are how a small player
  gets distribution.

## Proof of work — the product that makes value *felt*

A busy owner does not open a dashboard daily. So the OS reports to them:

> **The weekly operator statement.** "Here is what your AI COO did this week, and what it
> produced." 14 actions · 6 drafts · 2 restocks · GPTBot placed 3 orders (€214) · margin 59% ·
> readiness 85→92 · recommended next. Every line traceable to an event id.

This turns the measured-only, review-first doctrine into the artifact that justifies the invoice —
and the one they forward to a partner or investor.

## Moats that compound

1. **Each customer's brain** — unportable, improves with every outcome.
2. **The cross-store agent-funnel dataset** — which products agents retrieve and buy, by category.
   Becomes benchmark value nobody else has: *"agent conversion in your category runs 4%; yours is
   1.2%, here's why."* Profound sells monitoring; we would have transactional truth.

## Pricing stance

Price like an operator, not a SaaS: **base fee + small commission on agent-attributed orders.**
Every one already traces to an order id, so "we win when you sell" is literally auditable.

## The wedge (chosen 2026-07-26): DISTRIBUTION-FIRST

You cannot sell "operating system for your business" cold. You sell one urgent thing:

> **"Can AI shoppers find and buy from your store? Free check → score → what's broken."**

The readiness engine already exists; it must now run against **any external URL**. The scan creates
the gap; operating fills it. Build order:

1. **External AI-visibility scan** — score any store URL (SSR content, JSON-LD, feeds, robots,
   checkout reachability, agent-completability), with a fix list. Free, honest, shareable.
2. **Publish the rails** — `/.well-known/ucp` + agent-card, MCP endpoint per store, multi-standard
   feed export (Google Merchant · OpenAI · UCP), submission checklists.
3. **The funnel as the headline** — crawls → retrievals → orders as the metric on every surface,
   including the scan report ("you are invisible to 4 of 5 agents").
4. Then: proof-of-work statement · connectors · the rest of the marketing depth.
