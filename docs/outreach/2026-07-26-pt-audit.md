## Notes for whoever sends these

- **Lead with their number, not with us.** The score is the only thing that earns the second sentence.
- **Never overstate a zero.** For the bot-wall cases the honest claim is "your protection also blocks shopping agents", not "your site is broken".
- **The ask is the report, not a meeting.** Sending a real report to anyone who replies costs nothing and puts a document with our name on it in their inbox.
- **Re-run before sending.** These scores are from 2026-07-26.
- The strong one (Benamor) is worth sending precisely *because* it is strong — a near-perfect store with one honest gap is the easiest credibility win, and they are the likeliest to understand why the gap matters.
